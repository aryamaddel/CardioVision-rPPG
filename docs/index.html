Region traces

Two features

SPR — strength of the heart-rate spectral peak in dB. Coherence — do all regions show the same pulse?

REAL iff SPR ≥ 4 dB and coherence ≥ 0.35 and BPM is physiological (45–140).

Key finding. Neural swaps attack the pulse peak, not coherence: inswapper's feathered paste-back leaves the target's real skin — and its real pulse — at the face margins, pushing SPR to 19.8 dB, higher than any naive swap. Inter-region coherence (0.16 / 0.07) is what exposes them — reproducing FakeCatcher's central claim against a modern generator.

05 / THE FINE PRINT

Limitations & reproduction.

All numbers from committed run logs

What limits this result.

  • Landmark accuracy bounds the GPU path. The ONNX face mesh sits ~6–9 px from MediaPipe (ROI anchors: 4–5.5 px) and jitters 8–10 px per frame; a One-Euro filter cuts jitter 5× to ~2 px. Residual error is intrinsic to the model (~5–6 px) — and that is exactly what costs the one false negative.
  • Small dataset. Thresholds (4 dB, 0.35) are calibrated on these 2 subjects; more subjects would be needed to generalise.
  • Motion can fake coherence. In an earlier run, a talking-head clip whose head bobs on the beat passed both tests — the classic rPPG motion-artifact failure mode.

Synthetic fakes are generated from the dataset for research only.
